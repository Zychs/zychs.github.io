Decisions locked so we don't re-argue them
  • Formula: your norm wins. ETDI = √(v² + a² + s²) / (√3 × minutes) Flag threshold recalibrates to ≈ 0.30–0.35. Not yet in code.
  • Drift layer (v2): EMA baseline (η ≈ 0.9) + per-axis MAD σ + Euclidean norm of the normalized residual. Flag when D > τ ≈ 2 — "a two-sigma day for you." Collapses to your norm when no history exists.
  • 0.9.3 capture path: Python + sounddevice records for the alpha. The Zig engine stays the vision but does not gate shipping.
  • Voice control: push-to-talk, not wake-word. Boring and reliable ships; "hey sesefus" is a rabbit hole.
  • Testers without GPUs: heuristic backend by default, Ollama optional.
  • Positioning: drift instrument, not therapy app. "A mirror with a memory."

The pitch kit tap copy, say it out loud
One-liner
Sesefus is a voice journal that notices when you're drifting — from your sleep, your mood, your own baseline — before you do.
30 seconds
When I was in rehab they took my phone, and my days just blurred. I couldn't tell you what Tuesday felt like. So I built the thing I needed: you talk to it for sixty seconds a day — that's it, you just talk — and it learns what your normal sounds like. Then when you start sliding — sleep drifting, mood drifting, weeks starting to blur again — it shows you, quietly, before it costs you something. Everything stays on your own machine. No cloud, no subscription, no therapist branding. It's not therapy. It's a mirror with a memory.
Tester ask
I built a voice journal that catches you drifting before you notice it yourself. I need three people to talk to it for sixty seconds a day for two weeks and tell me where it's annoying. You'd be tester zero.
Technical (Quin, engineers)
Everything's local — Whisper and a 7B on my own GPU, encrypted SQLite vault, no cloud calls ever. Each memo becomes a point in emotion-space; I track your Mahalanobis drift off your own rolling baseline, so the flags are calibrated to you, not a population. The whole app is one loop: record actual, infer baseline, norm the residual, nudge.

Rules: lead with a person in a moment, never architecture. One drift per pitch (days blurring). World-building words stay in the repo: say "getting your days back," not "negentropic refinement." One company, one product: Neurialab builds drift-correction instruments; sesefus is the first.

Traps to remember from the fact-check
  • All six manifests have durationSeconds: null — paste a transcript without a duration and ETDI divides by a 3-second floor and inflates wildly.
  • --wav prints the score but never saves the transcript — nothing to copy into a manifest until the backfill flag exists.
  • Manifests already name their audio: read source.sourcePath, don't hunt by date.
  • 127.0.0.1:3001 root is a 404 — it's API-only. The face is the React UI at :5173 → sidebar → ETDI.
  • wav-* smoke rows linger in etdi.db and double-count against aje-* rows in the trend.
Ideas parked warm not lost, just waiting
  • Conversation sieve — third sibling: shred your saved AI chats into the same debris/vector pipeline, so the useful bits become searchable instead of guilt in a folder. Ingest needs zero changes.
  • Attention drift — weekly histogram of image-sieve concept tags vs your trailing baseline: "your hands wandered from what your mouth said mattered."
  • Cadence drift — scheduled journal time vs actual entry timestamps; data already exists in manifests.
  • Calibration drift — same math pointed at the model: LLM-inferred emotion vs occasional self-report. The most on-brand use of the RSDC name.
  • Salience gate fallback — if trivia rants pollute flags under the norm formula, s·√(v²+a²) returns as a filter, not a formula.
