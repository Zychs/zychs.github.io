Circadia

Keeps time for a person. Not a calendar. Not a lock screen. Not Artifact Scanner.

status · not done

What it is

A local host and client. One binary, two roles. The host ticks a 50 ms timekeeper. The client waits for a cue. A cue can play a sound or start a journal take.

The why is in the prologue: circadian entrainment when the phone is gone.

Honest now

  • Wired: alarm schedule, group, interval, toggle.
  • Stub: journal and rhythm print lines. ALIGNED is fake. Do not demo it.
  • Mic: Microsoft Sound Recorder. No custom widget.
  • Source: still in Sesefus core/sesephus/. This door does not pretend the extract already happened.
